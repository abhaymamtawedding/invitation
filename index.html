Dress Code & Attire

Cultural Fusion Theme: We welcome you to celebrate in both Kerala and Nepali traditions!

  • Gentlemen: Traditional Kerala Kasavu Mundu with Kurta/Shirt, Nepali Daura Suruwal with Dhaka Topi, or elegant ethnic/festive wear.
  • Ladies: Kerala Kasavu Saree / Set Mundu, rich red/maroon silk saree, Nepali Kurti/Lehenga, or colorful Indian ethnic attire.
💡 Light cottons or festive silks are ideal for Kerala’s coastal breeze.

The auspicious Muhurtham is strictly between 11:00 AM and 12:00 PM on Monday, 15 February 2027.

  • Comfortable sandals or slip-ons are recommended for walking on the beachfront garden pavilion.
🍛

The Sadhya Experience

The traditional Kerala wedding feast is an art form eaten with your fingers on a tapered fresh banana leaf:

  • The narrow tip of the banana leaf points to your left.
  • Over 20 distinct delicacies precede hot Matta rice, Parippu ghee, Sambar, and sweet Payasam.
  • Fold the leaf inwards towards yourself when satisfied!

Google Sheet Integration Guide

This invitation is configured to submit guest RSVPs and transportation requests directly to a Google Sheet via a free Google Apps Script Webhook.

  1. Create a new Google Sheet named "Abhay & Mamta Wedding RSVPs".
  2. Set up headers in Row 1: Timestamp | Name | Phone | Events | Guests | Attending | Transport | Pickup Location | Dietary Notes | Wishes.
  3. Click Extensions > Apps Script, paste the provided script from google_apps_script.js, and click Deploy > New Deployment > Web App.
  4. Set access to "Anyone" and copy the generated Web App URL.
  5. Paste the URL into script.js under GOOGLE_SHEET_WEBHOOK_URL.

Note: Until you plug in your custom URL, test submissions automatically succeed with local storage backup and instant WhatsApp dispatch!
